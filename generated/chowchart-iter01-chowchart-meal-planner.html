FAQ

The questions every RA asks us during welcome week.

Can I really stay under $50/week eating decent food?

Yes — our average user with a $50 cap actually spends $46.20/week and eats 21 meals plus snacks. The trick isn't cheaper food; it's overlapping ingredients across meals so nothing rots in your fridge. ChowChart does that math automatically.

What if I don't have a kitchen, just a microwave and a mini-fridge?

Toggle "microwave-only" in setup. We have 180+ recipes that need nothing but a microwave, a bowl, and a fork — including dorm-friendly oatmeal, burrito bowls, and steamed veggie packs.

Does it work with my campus meal swipes or EBT?

Both. Tell us how many dining-hall meals you have left per week and ChowChart only plans the gaps. EBT-eligible items are tagged in your grocery list so checkout is painless.

Which grocery stores do you support?

Aldi, Walmart, Kroger, H-E-B, Trader Joe's, Publix, Target, Whole Foods, and 90% of regional chains. We also support most college-town co-ops. If your store isn't on the list, you can plug in a generic price book and we'll still optimize.

Can I share a plan with my roommates?

Yes — Senior plan supports up to 4 roommates with separate diets and one shared cart. Costs split automatically based on what each person actually eats.

Is the AI going to suggest weird stuff?

You'll see normal student food: pasta, tacos, stir-fry, sandwiches, sheet-pan dinners, overnight oats. We trained the model on 40,000 actual student-submitted recipes, not Pinterest fantasy meals.
