FAQ

Questions students actually ask.

Is $50 a week actually realistic?
For most U.S. campuses, yes — if you're cooking ~14 meals a week and using your meal plan or leftovers for the rest. Our median user spends $44.80/week. In high-cost cities like NYC or San Francisco, expect closer to $58 unless you're hitting up Trader Joe's deals (which we surface).
I have literally just a microwave. Will this still work?
Yes. Toggle "no stove" in your kitchen profile and CheapEats will only suggest meals you can do with a microwave, kettle, and maybe a $20 hotplate. There are 800+ recipes that fit.
How does pricing actually work for the AI?
We pull from a database of weekly grocery flyers and crowdsourced receipts from 14,000+ stores, refreshed every 48 hours. The plan you see in the app should match within 5–8% of your actual checkout total.
What if I don't like a meal it suggests?
Tap "swap" and it'll regenerate a meal that uses the ingredients you've already bought, so nothing goes to waste. After about 2 weeks of swaps, it stops suggesting things you don't like.
Is my .edu required?
Only for the discounted Student plan. The Free tier doesn't require any verification. We accept any active U.S. or Canadian university email — including community colleges.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. One tap in settings. We refund unused months. No "we kept charging you all summer break" nonsense — we auto-pause billing during summer if you tell us you're going home.
